PayBeacon is made by one person. Every message is read; replies usually come within 7 days. When you report a problem, please give your iPhone model, iOS version, the app language and the steps that show it.

Questions

Can PayBeacon cancel a subscription for me?

No app can do that for you. Marking one Cancel records your decision and stops counting it; the detail screen links to the service's own page and to the App Store's subscriptions page, where you stop it for real.

Where are my photos and dates kept?

On your iPhone only, in the app's own folder. Settings lets you export your data as plain text or erase all of it.

Why is the price field empty in my currency?

Catalog prices are US dollars and the app never converts. In any other currency you type what you actually pay.

Does it need the internet?

No. It works offline, reads photos on the phone, and sends nothing anywhere.
