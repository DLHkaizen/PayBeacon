Privacy Policy

PayBeacon collects no data. No account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no networking code.

  • The subscriptions, due dates, groups and settings you enter stay on your iPhone.
  • Photos of bills and warranty cards are kept in the app's own folder on your iPhone, are read on the phone, and are deleted with their item or by Reset the app.
  • The camera is used only when you tap Camera. Choosing a photo uses the system picker and gives the app only the photo you pick.
  • Reminders are scheduled by your iPhone, only after you turn them on.
  • Anything leaves your iPhone only when you tap Export, to the place you choose.
  • Deleting the app, or Reset the app, removes everything. There is no copy anywhere else.
