Subscriptions and due dates

See what your subscriptions cost every month and every year, and never miss a due date again: car inspection, insurance, a warranty, the last day to return something.

An app for iPhone, on the App Store

Every subscription, one total

Streaming, music, AI tools, cloud storage, the gym: sorted by price, with your monthly total at the top and the yearly figure beneath.

Four equal decisions

Keep, Pause, Cancel or Later, four buttons of the same size. The savings card counts the charges that did not happen.

Every date you must not miss

Vehicle inspection, insurance, a warranty, the last day to return something, a passport. Several reminders per item; a yearly one moves to next year when done.

Read from a photo

Photograph the bill or the warranty card. The phone reads the dates itself and fills in the form; the photo stays with the item.

No account, no network

Everything stays on your iPhone. No ads, no tracking, nothing sent anywhere.
